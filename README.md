This is the home of [joinakari.com](https://joinakari.com/), the website for Akari.

## What Akari does

Most health apps hand you numbers. Akari tells you what they mean.

It brings your sleep, movement and food together in one place. Each morning you see how ready you are, measured against your own usual nights rather than someone else's average. Logging a meal is as easy as writing, saying or photographing it, and every nutrient gets its own row. You start with one goal, like sleeping better or moving more, and go from there.

## Your data stays yours

There's no account to create. Akari reads Apple Health on your iPhone and keeps your health data there. A meal you type, say or photograph is read by an AI service so Akari can work out what's in it, and none of it is stored. The only thing that ever reaches us is a food report you choose to send, so we can fix a food Akari got wrong. The [privacy policy](https://joinakari.com/privacy/) has every detail.

## Try it

Akari is in a free public beta on [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/wrv4aFVQ), with everything unlocked. The App Store version is coming soon.

You'll need an iPhone with iOS 26 or later. A fitness tracker that writes to Apple Health, like an Apple Watch, adds sleep and heart rate, but it's optional: without one, Akari still works with your iPhone's steps and the food you log. Akari speaks English and German.

Akari explains your own data and patterns. It isn't medical advice, so please talk to your doctor about health concerns.
