FAQ

Good to know

Akari is free during beta and available for iPhone through Apple’s TestFlight. Here is what to know before you try it.

What is Akari?

Akari is an iPhone app that brings your sleep, movement and food together in one place and explains in plain words what they mean for your day.

What does Akari cost?

Most of Akari is free. Food logging, readiness and Health Age need a subscription: €4.99 a month or €39.99 a year, with 14 days free to try and no commitment. During the beta everything is free.

Does my health data leave my iPhone?

No. Akari needs no account and keeps your health data on your iPhone. Only a food report you choose to send reaches us, so we can fix the match.

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later and Apple Health. Akari speaks English and German.

Is Akari medical advice?

No. Akari explains your own data and patterns. It does not diagnose or treat anything, so talk to your doctor about health concerns.

How can I try Akari?

Join the free beta through Apple TestFlight. The App Store version is coming soon.
