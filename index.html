FAQ 💬

Akari is free during beta and available for iPhone through Apple’s TestFlight. Here is what to know before you try it.

What is Akari?
Akari is an iPhone health app that turns Apple Health data into a calm, readable daily story.
What health data can I see?
Akari brings supported vitals, activity, nutrition and goals together so you can understand your day in context.
Does my health data leave my iPhone?
No. Akari works without an account, and your Apple Health data stays on your iPhone.
How can I try Akari?
Join the free public beta through Apple TestFlight and help shape the app before launch.
