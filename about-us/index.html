About us

Why Akari exists

Your iPhone already knows a lot about how you sleep, move and eat. Most health apps turn that into scores and streaks to keep up with, and want an account and your data in return.

We wanted an app that simply tells you what it all means for your day, in plain words, and then lets you get on with it. So we made Akari.

We’re two people in Germany. We work out every idea for Akari together and then split the rest: Rafael designs and builds the app, and Jan, a lawyer, researches the science behind it and checks the facts and the words.

You’ll notice that in how Akari talks to you. Its explanations rest on research, and they’re written so anyone can follow them, without jargon and without alarm.
